What people ask before they call.

What do you actually do?

We run your organization's managed digital workspace: the desktops your people work on, the security around them, and the support when something breaks. Inside the scope we agree in writing, we own the outcome. One partner, one contract. We work with healthcare, financial services and legal organizations.

What happens to our files when you rebuild desktops?

They stay. Profiles and files live in separate, managed storage, so people sign in to a fresh desktop with their work where they left it. Only the desktops get rebuilt.

Which frameworks do you support?

On Advanced, our workspace helps support CIS Controls v8.1, NIST CSF 2.0 and HIPAA. On Enterprise, it helps support SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001 and HITRUST. Core does not include framework support. We provide the technical controls. You own your compliance program and your auditor.

Who can reach our environment?

Your tenant belongs to you and is separate from ours. Daily administration runs through Microsoft's delegated admin privileges. Access is tiered: no named Trisian user holds standing global administrator rights in your tenant, higher access levels are activated just in time for a few hours at most, and emergency break-glass access is sealed, monitored and logged.

Can we see your security documentation?

Yes, in two releases. The Security Overview, the Security Whitepaper, the ISO/IEC 27001 verification link and the list of main subprocessors are on our Trust Center at trust.trisian.com, with no gate. The Statement of Applicability, policy summaries, incident response and continuity summaries, and control mappings are shared through the Trust Center on request, after an NDA. Nothing is claimed before the document that proves it is issued.

How do we contract?

Through three documents: the Master Services Agreement, the Quote and the Services Guide. Together they set the scope, the plan, the price per user, the support hours package and the service levels, so the terms can be read in advance. They are reviewed by counsel and change only after counsel's review.

How does pricing work?

Four components. A per-user monthly platform fee that covers everything automated. A monthly minimum package of support hours for the human work, every hour logged. Projects, scoped and quoted separately. And optional add-ons, each with a licence and a one-time implementation. The Microsoft 365 licence and Azure are passed through at cost: Azure at exactly what your Azure portal shows, with no markup.

Do you replace our IT team?

No. We work with your team, not around it. Inside what we manage, we own the outcome. Your team keeps the context, and we take on the load.

How do we know which plan fits?

We work it out together on the first call. We look at your size, your industry, your compliance needs and your hours, and recommend a starting point. Every plan starts at 50 users; Core runs to 300.

What happens outside business hours?

Core support runs Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, your local time. On Advanced and Enterprise, critical and high-impact incidents are covered 24/7; other requests run in the same business-hours window. Automated platform monitoring, patching and alerting run 24/7/365 on every plan. Core clients who need occasional out-of-hours cover can add an emergency readiness retainer, and an incident response retainer is available on every plan. Response commitments are set in the Services Guide that forms part of your agreement.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on your environment. We scope the timeline with you up front, so you get a realistic plan, not a generic promise.

Can you move us off Citrix or an on-premises setup?

Yes. We migrate you from Citrix and from on-premises VDI to Azure Virtual Desktop, keeping your profiles and settings. And if you must keep desktops in your own datacenter, Safe Workspace is also delivered on Citrix. Migrations are scoped and quoted as projects.

Do you support our applications?

We manage the infrastructure and operating system your applications run on. Application-level support stays with you or your software vendor. Our service desk is staffed Level 1 to 3.

Where does our data live, and how fast can we recover?

Your tenant belongs to you and is separate from ours, and your data is hosted in the region its regulation requires; for US clients, that is US Azure regions. Disaster recovery is designed as a project, to recovery targets agreed in writing. Microsoft 365 data relies on Microsoft's native retention, with granular recovery available as an add-on.
